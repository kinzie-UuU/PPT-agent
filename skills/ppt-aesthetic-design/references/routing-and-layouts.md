# Routing And Layouts

## Deck Types

- Sales battlecard: product, price, objections, comparison, bundles, risks.
- Client proposal: client problem, offer, evidence, plan, cost, next action.
- Product introduction: product story, features, use cases, specs, proof, pricing.
- Data report: KPI, findings, comparison, timeline, implications.
- Old deck redesign: source-preserving diagnosis and reconstruction.
- Lightweight material整理: clear reading order from sparse notes.

## Page Count

- Respect explicit user choices such as 8, 12, or 19 pages.
- System recommendation: sparse material -> 8 pages; sufficient product/price material -> 12 pages; old PPT or 15+ page PDF -> 19-page full route.

## Layout Rules

- First page: `cover`.
- Last page: `closing` or `quote`.
- 10+ pages: insert `toc`.
- Price bands: include at least one `pricing`.
- Product candidates: include `product-detail` and `cards`.
- 3+ products or price tiers: add `compare` or `bundle`.
- Image material: include `visual` and write image slots.
- Risk list: keep `risk-checklist` unless user explicitly cancels it.

## Rhythm

- 5+ pages: at least 3 layout types.
- 8+ pages: at least 5 layout types.
- Never use the same body layout 3 times in a row.
- Insert `section`, `quote`, or `visual` as breathing pages in long decks.

## Density

- Respect each layout's bullet limit.
- Split pages when content exceeds limits.
- Rewrite long paragraphs into headline plus evidence.
- Do not solve density by shrinking text below readable size.
